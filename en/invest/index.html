How we calculate
  • Appreciation: the price grows at the annual rate you enter, compounded monthly, up to the date considered.
  • Gross revenue: nightly rate × 365 nights × occupancy. E.g. R$ 400 × 365 × 50% = R$ 73,000 a year.
  • Net revenue: gross revenue minus management and platform costs (as % of revenue) and annual fixed costs. Rental only starts after delivery.
  • Selling at delivery ends the calculation at delivery. Keep and rent adds, in reais, the estimated capital gain and net rental income over the stated horizon. We never add rates from different periods.
  • Instalments, financing, transfer tax (ITBI), income taxes and selling costs are not included.

Brazilians abroad and foreigners

Guide for foreign investors

Draft pending legal and tax review before publication.

1. CPF

The CPF is the Brazilian taxpayer number required to sign contracts and the deed. Non-residents can request it at a Brazilian consulate or through the Federal Revenue channels.

2. Sending funds

Money should come in through a bank or exchange broker authorised by the Central Bank, with an exchange contract stating the purpose. Keep the receipts: they document the source of funds.

3. Off-plan purchase and deed

Off-plan, you sign a purchase commitment with the developer. The deed is executed at a notary on full payment and registered at the Property Registry, with ITBI transfer tax paid to the municipality. Signing by power of attorney is possible.

4. Rental and taxes

Rental income and any capital gain follow specific rules for residents and non-residents. Talk to an accountant before buying.
